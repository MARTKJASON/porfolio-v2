How a request moves

Members create a request for their equipment. An Admin does the first-level review, then a Manager makes the final decision. A request at the Pending Manager stage can be approved or rejected there.

Member creates request→ Pending Admin→ Pending Manager→ Approved / Rejected

What the system does

01

Role-based access

Member, Admin and Manager roles, each with its own permissions and views.

02

Equipment workflow

Members manage assigned equipment and raise requests that go through two-level approval.

03

HRIS employee sync

Employee records are kept in step with the HR system instead of being entered by hand.

04

Asset imports

Bulk import of assets, so a whole inventory goes in at once.

05

Facility Management Sheet

A downloadable .xlsx report of facilities and equipment for management.

06

Employee management

Admin tools to manage employees and what is assigned to them.
