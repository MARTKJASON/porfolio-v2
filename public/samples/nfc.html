An NFC product for businesses that does more than share a link. Each tap point, like the counter, a table or the receipt, goes to its own URL, so one card can collect Google reviews, send people to Facebook or capture leads.

The idea

Most NFC cards point to a single link. Giving each touchpoint its own path lets a business change where a tap goes at any time, and see which spot in the store gets people to act.

Customer taps card→ Touchpoint URL→ Dynamic redirect→ Review · Follow · Lead

What I explored

01

Touchpoint architecture

One path for each place a customer is: counter, tables, receipt and takeout.

02

Dynamic redirects

The card stays the same while its destination can change, with no reprinting.

03

Review & social links

Tap-to-phone shortcuts to Google Reviews and Facebook pages.

04

NTAG215 hardware

Choosing and programming NFC chips that work on everyday phones.

05

Lead generation

Turning a tap into a customer interaction the business can follow up on.

06

Reselling

Looking at reselling NFC cards as a product line of its own.

Product thinking, not just code

This project is as much about the business as the build: who the customer is, what a tap should achieve and how a small business can use it every day.
